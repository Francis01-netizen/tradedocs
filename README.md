# TradeDocs

> The future of trader development starts with better records.

TradeDocs is a trader development platform designed to help traders document decisions, analyze performance, and improve through structured review and intelligent insights.

---

## Vision

Most traders study the markets.

Few study themselves.

TradeDocs exists to help traders transform decisions into data, and data into growth through journaling, performance analytics, and continuous improvement.

What begins as a modern trading journal is intended to evolve into a complete trader development platform.
